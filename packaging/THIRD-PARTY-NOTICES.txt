Third-party software in this build of Composa

Composa itself is MIT licensed; see LICENSE.

The Windows and macOS builds of Composa include Magick.NET, which is how they open HEIC, AVIF, TIFF and camera RAW files without ImageMagick being installed separately. Linux builds include only the managed Magick.NET.Core ICC profile resources and use the ImageMagick that the distribution provides for conversions.

Magick.NET is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. It carries ImageMagick (ImageMagick License) and the libraries ImageMagick uses to read and write image formats, all compiled into the single native library Magick.Native-Q16-HDRI-<architecture>.dll (or .dylib on macOS) that sits next to the Composa executable. Every component, its version and its full licence text is listed in ImageMagick-NOTICE.txt, which is Magick.NET's own notice file, shipped unchanged.

Several of those components are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, among them libraw (camera RAW), libheif and libde265 (HEIC). Composa does not modify them. The native library is loaded at runtime as a separate file, so it can be replaced with one built from source. The sources and the build scripts that produce it are published at:

  https://github.com/dlemstra/Magick.Native
  https://github.com/dlemstra/Magick.NET

and each component's own upstream source is linked from ImageMagick-NOTICE.txt.

Film looks

Every build of Composa bundles four film looks for the Color Lookup adjustment, derived from sguyader/FilmSim (https://github.com/sguyader/FilmSim) at commit 1453b2b55c48d99a889b1e455f91f6898ba2db41, authored by Sebastien Guyader and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 asks for nothing; this record is a courtesy and lets anyone re-derive and verify the files. The sources are 144-point HaldCLUT TIFF images, each resampled to a 33-point .cube by scripts/make-looks.sh:

  Muted Chrome    from Classic_Chrome.tif   SHA-256 cc303723b76205aedf970dc814dbecd6d9795955411aa2d6b265873864de7a66

  Vivid Slide     from Velvia_Vivid_v2.tif  SHA-256 1b347b4c5afec78ad7f79103bf04b319e182b7133ffe2a8864c732a7003b7eea

The source files are named after Fujifilm film stocks. Those names are trademarks of Fujifilm, which the CC0 dedication does not touch, so Composa ships the looks under descriptive names and shows the stock names nowhere but here and in each file's provenance comment, as a record of origin.

On-device models

Every build of Composa includes ONNX Runtime and three models: two segmentation models, which is how Select > Subject, the Object Selection tool and Remove Background find the subject of an ordinary photo on your own machine, and one enlarging model, which is what Enhance in Image Size runs. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is downloaded at run time.

ONNX Runtime (https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation, is licensed under the MIT License and arrives through the Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime NuGet package, version 1.30.0. Its native library (libonnxruntime.so, onnxruntime.dll or libonnxruntime.dylib) sits next to the Composa executable; its own LICENSE and ThirdPartyNotices.txt are in that package and in its repository.

The models are not source code and are not covered by Composa's MIT licence; each carries the licence of its own project, which covers the trained weights as well as the code. Composa ships the community ONNX conversions that Lolly (github.com/lolly-tools/lolly) also uses, pinned by the SHA-256 of the exact file, so anyone can verify what is in a build. They are in the models folder beside the executable.

Compositor algorithms

TextureInpaint.cs adapts multi-scale texture matching from Rendering/PatchPixels.c at the same pinned commit below. Its seam adaptation uses Composa's existing push-pull solver.
ShapePaths.cs and Scanlines.cs are adapted from robbietilton/Compositor v1.5.3, commit 554104cf1c2de844c1acbd60b4f7b422bd6ce7bd, Document/ShapeTool.swift and Rendering/DitherPixels.c.
CameraRawTables.cs, CameraRawShadows.cs, CameraRawMeasuredColor.cs and the monotone parametric curve are adapted from Document/CameraRawTables.swift, Document/CameraRawShadows.swift, Document/CameraRawColor.swift and Rendering/AdjustPixels.c at that same commit.
CameraRawData contains the original CameraRawShadows.bin and a lossless gzip repack of CameraRawTables.bin. scripts/import-compositor-tables.py records their original SHA-256 hashes and reproduces the repack without a runtime LZMA dependency.
DeepFilters.cs and DeepAdjustments.cs adapt precision reconstruction from Rendering/DeepPixels.c. DeepPixels.cs adapts the immutable source/painted-preview approach and per-channel noise from Document/DeepLayers.swift and Rendering/DeepPixels.c at that same commit.
